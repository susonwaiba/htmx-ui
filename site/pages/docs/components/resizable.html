Markup

  • A .resizable group with data-resizable holds .resizable-panel elements with data-resizable-panel, separated by .resizable-handle elements with data-resizable-handle. Give each handle an aria-label; the behaviour makes it a focusable role="separator" and keeps aria-valuenow (the size of the panel before it) up to date.
  • Sizes are percentages of the group: data-size="25". Panels without one share what is left. data-min and data-max bound a panel (default 0 and 100).
  • data-orientation="vertical" stacks the panels; give the group a height. Groups nest: a panel can hold another group, and each handle only resizes the panels on either side of it.
  • Arrow keys move a focused handle by 5% (with Shift, 1%); Home and End go to the limits. Double-click a handle to restore the starting sizes.
  • While dragging, iframes in the group stop catching the pointer, so a drag over a preview keeps tracking.

Collapse a panel

A data-collapsible panel closes when it is dragged under half its minimum, or with Enter on its handle. A <button data-resizable-toggle="panel-id"> anywhere on the page closes and reopens a panel by id, and gets aria-expanded and aria-controls. The space goes to the panel next to it, so the others stay put, and the panel reopens at the size it had.

Events

The group fires resizable:change after every resize, collapse or reopen, with detail.sizes (percentages, 0 for collapsed panels) and detail.collapsed (their ids).
